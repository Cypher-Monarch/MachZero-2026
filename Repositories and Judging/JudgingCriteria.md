## Round 4: Finale (Total: /25)
* **Merge-Readiness & Upstream Compliance** · out of 5 

How close the PR is to being accepted by maintainers without major rewrites. Does it strictly follow the target repo's code style, architecture patterns, and contribution guidelines? Any breaking changes, license/legal hurdles, or unnecessary dependencies?

* **Functional Correctness & Rigorous Validation** · out of 5

Whether the code or prototype actually delivers on its promise and holds up under scrutiny. Software: do automated tests pass, and does it handle edge cases? Hardware: are there concrete, measured results (benchmark data, signal integrity, physical tolerances) proving it functions reliably?

* **Impact & Value to the Project** · out of 5

The significance of the contribution relative to the effort. How meaningful and useful is this change to the project's users or maintainers? Does it solve a critical bug, remove a major bottleneck, or introduce a highly desired feature that adds long-term utility?

* **Code Quality, Design, & Maintainability** · out of 5

The craftsmanship of the implementation beneath the surface. Is the code clean, modular, efficient, and well-commented? Is the design future-proof, avoiding quick hacks that create technical debt for future maintainers?

* **PR Artifact Quality & Presentation Clarity** · out of 5

The team's ability to communicate their work to maintainers and stakeholders. Is the PR description comprehensive (why the change was made, how it was implemented, how to test it)? How clearly and convincingly did the team present their PR on the main stage?
