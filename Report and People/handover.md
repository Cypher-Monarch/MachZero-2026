### Publishing this repo

When the report is final and you want a public version:

1. Create a new public repo(or fork this one) and remove anything with personal data or unrelated.
2. Keep: `README.md`, `announcements/`, `event/`, `people/` (names only, with consent), `report/`
3. Re-check with a search for "password", "@", "+91" before pushing
4. Turn on GitHub Pages from `main` if you want a simple site


.

Dont forget to thank the participants, judges, sponsors, faculty and finally, yourselves! Best of Luck :D
